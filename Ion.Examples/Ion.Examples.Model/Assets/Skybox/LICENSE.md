# Skybox

A procedural sky (horizon to zenith gradient, a sun low in the -Z direction, 27 degrees up, a darker ground) rendered into six 128x128
cube map faces (`px`, `nx`, `py`, `ny`, `pz`, `nz`) for the Ion model sample. Made for Ion and dedicated to the public
domain under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode).
